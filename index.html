Trading sessions

Times shown in your local time and adjust automatically for daylight saving in London, New York and Sydney. Gold and forex move most during the London–New York overlap; the Asian session is usually quieter.

High-impact US news

Gold, EUR/USD, GBP/USD and BTC can jump sharply on these releases. The signal switches to WAIT from 30 minutes before until 30 minutes after each one. Dates: Federal Reserve FOMC calendar and BLS CPI schedule; NFP is shown on the first Friday of each month, which is its usual slot, so confirm on an economic calendar.

Backtest on the loaded candles

Replays the same rules over every loaded candle: enter at the close when a signal fires, stop 1.5 × ATR, target 2 × the stop, exit after 50 candles if neither is hit, one trade at a time. At 1:2 risk-reward you need to win more than 33% of trades to make money before costs. Spread, commission and slippage are not included, and the multi-timeframe and news filters are not applied in this replay.

Position size

Lot size = money at risk ÷ (stop distance × contract size). Check your broker's contract size before trading.

Read this before trading. The bull/bear figure is an indicator-agreement score mapped to a 20–80% range; the backtest win rate is the better guide, and a few hundred candles is still a small sample. no indicator set predicts any market reliably. Live prices come from free public exchange feeds (Kraken, Binance) that allow browser access: gold uses PAXG, a token backed 1:1 by gold, and EUR/USD and GBP/USD use exchange FX rates. These track the real market closely but can differ slightly, and they keep trading on weekends when spot gold and forex are closed. Test on a demo account first. In India, trading XAUUSD, EUR/USD or GBP/USD with offshore forex brokers is not permitted under RBI rules; MCX, NSE currency futures and gold ETFs are the legal routes.
How the score is built

Each indicator casts a vote from −1 (sell) to +1 (buy), multiplied by its weight: EMA stack 2, price vs EMA 200 2, MACD 1.5, RSI 1, Stochastic 1, ADX direction 1.5, Bollinger position 1, Supertrend 1.5, momentum (ROC 10) 1. The weighted total is divided by the maximum possible score to give −100% to +100%. A signal fires only when the score passes ±35% and ADX shows a trend (above 20). It is then downgraded to WAIT if the two higher timeframes do not lean the same way (score at least +10% for a buy, −10% for a sell) or if high-impact US news is within 30 minutes. During quiet Asian-only hours the signal is kept but flagged as low liquidity. Stops are 1.5 × ATR(14) from the last close; targets are 1× and 2× that distance.
